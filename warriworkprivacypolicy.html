Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Warriwork ("Company," "we," "us," "our," or "App"). Warriwork is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (the "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

Workout Data:

  • Exercise names and types
  • Sets, reps, and weight tracking
  • Workout session dates and times
  • Custom workout programs you create
  • Workout history and completion status

Device Information:

  • Device type (iOS, Android, or Web)
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Device identifier (only for crash reporting and analytics)

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Analytics Data:

  • App usage patterns
  • Feature usage frequency
  • Performance metrics
  • Error and crash logs

Storage:

We use AsyncStorage (device-local storage) to persist your workout data on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service - Track your workouts and fitness progress
  • To improve the Service - Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and user experience
  • To ensure security - Detect and prevent fraudulent activity and technical issues
  • To send notifications - Provide app updates and service-related announcements (if applicable)
  • To personalize your experience - Deliver customized workout programs and recommendations
  • Legal compliance - Fulfill legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All your workout data is stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage. Your data does not leave your device unless you explicitly choose to export or sync it.

4.2 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 No Sale of Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

5.2 When We May Share Information

We may disclose your information when:

  • Required by law, court order, or government request
  • Necessary to protect our legal rights or safety
  • Authorized by you
  • With service providers under confidentiality agreements

5.3 Data Deletion

When you delete the app or clear your local data, your workout history is removed from your device. We do not retain copies of your data on our servers.

6. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Portability

You have the right to:

  • Access all your personal data stored in the app
  • Export your workout data (if applicable)
  • Request a copy of your information

6.2 Deletion

You can:

  • Delete individual workout sessions
  • Clear all app data by uninstalling the app
  • Request permanent deletion of any personal information

6.3 Opt-Out Options

  • You may opt out of analytics collection through app settings
  • You can disable push notifications in your device settings
  • You can deny app permissions (camera, location, etc.) in your device settings

7. Children's Privacy

Warriwork is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete such information immediately.

Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided information to Warriwork should contact us immediately.

8. Data Retention

  • Active Data: Your workout data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Deleted Data: Once deleted, data is permanently removed from your device
  • Backup Data: We recommend regularly backing up important data
  • Server Data: We do not retain server-side copies of your data

9. International Data Transfers

If you access Warriwork from outside your country, your data may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from your home country. By using Warriwork, you consent to the transfer of your data to countries outside your country of residence.

10. Third-Party Links

Warriwork may contain links to third-party websites and applications. This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party services, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing your information.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Know: You can request what personal information we have collected
  • Right to Delete: You can request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at the information provided in Section 13.

12. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request access to your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your data
  • Right to Restriction: Restrict processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 13.
